# HomeServiceHub Knowledge Base

## What is HomeServiceHub?

HomeServiceHub is a live marketplace for home services. Customers post tasks they need done — such as plumbing, electrical work, painting, cleaning, carpentry, and HVAC — and nearby verified workers apply to do the job. The platform handles matching, messaging, scheduling, and secure payment, so there is no phone tag, no cash, and no surprises.

## How does booking work?

Booking is easy. Scroll to the Services section, pick what you need, and tap Get started. You will choose a time slot, set a budget if you want, and submit the request. Nearby workers in the right category see your task and apply if interested. You can review applicants, message them, and confirm the worker you want before the job begins.

## What services are available?

We offer plumbing, electrical work, painting, cleaning, carpentry, and HVAC. Browse them all under the Services section on the homepage. Each category is a live queue of tasks waiting to be claimed.

## How do payments work?

Payments are handled securely after the job is done. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, or bank transfer. You will see the total price before confirming, and the amount is held safely and released to the worker only once you approve the completed work.

## How do I cancel a booking?

You can cancel any booking up to 2 hours before the scheduled time with no charge. Go to your messages with the worker and tap Cancel task. If you need to cancel inside the 2-hour window, contact the worker directly through the app to arrange.

## How do I message a worker?

Open your active task and tap the message button. This opens a direct chat with the assigned or applying worker. You can confirm arrival, share details about the job, and follow progress until it is done.

## How do workers get matched?

Workers build their profile with their trade, service area, hourly rate, and availability. They browse open tasks in their category filtered by distance and budget, then apply with one tap. Customers review applications and choose the worker they want.

## What verification do workers pass?

Every worker passes ID and background verification before joining. Each worker also has ratings on every job, so you can see their track record before hiring.

## Is there a subscription?

No subscription. Customers pay only when a job is done, and workers are paid the moment a task completes.

## How do I report a problem?

If something goes wrong with a task, worker, or payment, you can submit a complaint from your dashboard. Our support team reviews it and gets back to you.
